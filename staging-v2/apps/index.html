Questions

What is a web app?

Software that runs in a web browser. Your team opens a link and signs in, on a phone, a laptop or the computer at the front desk. Nothing to install or update.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. Everything we build is made for small screens as well as big ones.

Can it work with what we already use?

Often, yes: email, payments, calendars and spreadsheets. We check what yours can connect to before we promise anything.

Do you run one yourselves?

Yes. The contact form on our home page files every inquiry straight into our own back office, built the same way.

What does it cost?

Each app is priced for the job, after we have talked it through. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a plan.
