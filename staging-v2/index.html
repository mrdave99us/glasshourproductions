Questions

What is Glass Hour?

A development studio with a shelf. We build websites, apps and brand work for clients, and we publish templates, scenes and graphics that anyone can pick up and use.

What is on the shelf?

Website templates, 360 scenes for backdrops and virtual sets, and graphics for building websites: backgrounds, textures, product backdrops, icons and seasonal banners. Motion graphics are in the works. We are hand-picking the first pieces now, so the shelf fills as we go.

Is the stock made with AI?

Some of it. Scenes and graphics marked AI were generated and finished in our studio. Website templates and custom work are designed and built by hand.

How do I buy?

Two ways: one piece at a time, or a subscription that covers everything. The store is not open yet, so for now write to us and we will get you what you need.

Can you build something just for me?

Yes. That is the other half of what we do. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a plan.
